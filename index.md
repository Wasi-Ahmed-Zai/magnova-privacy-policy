# Privacy Policy — Magnova Clinic Chatbot Demo

**Last updated:** October 15, 2026

## 1. About This Demo

Magnova Clinic Chatbot Demo is a demonstration project developed by Magnova Enterprises to test WhatsApp messaging and AI-powered customer support. It is not an official chatbot operated by Karmani Dental Care or any other clinic.

## 2. Information We Process

During testing, the application may receive WhatsApp messages, sender identifiers, message timestamps, and related technical information through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform.

Please do not send real patient records, medical histories, passwords, or other sensitive personal information to this demo.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to test message delivery, chatbot responses, and technical functionality. Test messages and webhook payloads may appear in development logs.

## 4. Third-Party Services

This demo uses Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform and may use Google Gemini through the OpenAI Agents SDK integration to generate responses. These services may process information as required to provide their functionality and are subject to their own privacy policies and terms.

## 5. Data Retention and Security

This is a development demo and is not intended for production use with real customer or patient data. Test data may remain in temporary logs during development. Appropriate steps will be taken to remove test data when it is no longer needed.

## 6. Medical Disclaimer

The chatbot is not a healthcare professional and does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or prescriptions. Medical questions should be directed to a qualified healthcare professional.
