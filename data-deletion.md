## How to Request Data Deletion

If you have sent messages to Magnova Clinic Chatbot Demo and want your information deleted, please email:

**Email:** wasizai60@gmail.com

Use the subject line **Data Deletion Request** and include your WhatsApp phone number with your country code.

## What Happens Next?

1. We will review your request.
2. We will identify any related test messages or development logs that we control.
3. We will delete the relevant data we control, where applicable.
4. We will respond to your request by email.

Some information may remain with third-party services, such as Meta, according to their own data policies and retention practices.
